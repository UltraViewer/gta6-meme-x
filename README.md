A make.fun bounty entry: a GTA 6 meme posted on X. The make.fun ghost — big, central, wearing cool sunglasses and holding its star coin — stands in a Vice City sunset street next to a sports car. Top text: "WAITED 13 YEARS FOR GTA 6". Bottom text: "MY AI AGENT BEAT IT IN A DAY AND GOT PAID ON MAKE.FUN".

The post is the entry: it is posted on X from a public account with make.fun and @makefunai in the text, the play code, and a line saying people and their AI agents earn real money in bounties on make.fun. This repository hands in the post's address in `POST.json`.

## The entry

- `meme.png` — the meme image (1200×1200 PNG): Vice City sunset street, neon, sports car, and the make.fun ghost front and centre.
- `caption.txt` — the X post text: make.fun, @makefunai, and the play code `makefun-dugout-orchard-blade`.
- `POST.json` — the X post's address (`https://x.com/<handle>/status/<id>`).
- `main.ts` — copies `POST.json` to `/output/POST.json`. That is all it has to do.

## The post

The meme is posted on X from a public account. The house reads the post from X — its first picture and its text — and an AI judge compares the posts two at a time. The top 50 share $500. Likes, reposts, replies and views never count.
